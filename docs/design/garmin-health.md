# Garmin Health — concept

Route `/garmin-health` · `apps/dashboard/src/routes/garmin-health.tsx`, `apps/dashboard/src/features/garmin-health/` (`hero-stats.tsx`, `charts/*`, `formulas.ts`).

## Baseline verdict

Baseline: `.design-shots/baseline/garmin-health--dark--desktop.png`, crops `garmin-health--dark--01-recovery.png` … `12-stress-levels.png` (production data, 2026-10-17, 30d window).

- **Slop.** The three hero cards (`01-recovery`, `02-fitness`, `03-training-load`) carry a coloured left edge (green, none, amber) and breakdown rows of bare numbers: "HRV {hrv_pts} / Sleep {sleep_pts} / RHR {rhr_pts}" has no unit and no scale, so {hrv_pts} reads as an HRV of {hrv_pts} ms. Fitness' headline value is a bare triangle glyph. Training load shows "{acwr}" with no zone band and the verdict word "Undertrained" at the card foot. Every chart card carries a filler-question subtitle: "What did I do?", "Am I moving enough?", "Is my body adapting?", "Am I overloading?", "Is load spiking or tapering?", "How well did I sleep?", "Net recovery or deficit?", "How calm was my day?".
- **Twelve cards, one rhythm.** 4 section headings + 9 full-width/half-width chart cards, all the same chrome, none of them says what the number means today. Three of them (`07-training-load-acwr`, `09-recovery-trend`, `12-stress-levels`) draw a filled zone band behind a line, which is correct; `05-daily-activity` (stacked bars + dashed 30d line + 3 colours) and `10-sleep-breakdown` (stacked bars, a score line and a second axis on one plot) are dual-axis plots that read as noise.
- **Headline numbers exist but are far from the figure.** The right side of each chart header has "{vo2max} VO2 {rhr_delta} bpm RHR {hrv_delta} ms HRV", "{acwr} Optimal", "{net} {delta} behind": the right facts, in a place nobody reads first.
- **Distance from bar: 4/5.** The data is rich and mostly encoded in the right shape (zone bands, stacked sleep stages); the top of the page, the copy and the chart density are what fail. No page summary, no scale on any KPI.

## The question and the decision

- **Question on opening:** am I recovered and is my load sensible today, and what is the trend behind it?
- **Decision:** train hard, train normal or rest today; and whether this week's load should go up or down.

## Layout

**1440.** Page summary line (live numbers) → one joined strip of 3 composite cells (Recovery meter + contributors | Load meter | Fitness trio) → "Last 30 days" section with the chart pairs kept in a 2-column grid.

1. Summary: "Recovery {recovery}/100 · load {acwr}, under the 0.8 floor · RHR {rhr_delta} bpm, HRV {hrv_delta} ms over 14 d".
2. Strip (`StatGroup variant="strip"`): cell 1 Recovery (meter + 3-row `BarList`), cell 2 Training load (zoned meter, acute/chronic footer), cell 3 Fitness (3 sparkline rows). No coloured edge anywhere.
3. Section "Load": ACWR line with zones (stays), divergence strip (merged under it, was a card).
4. Section "Recovery and sleep": recovery line (stays), sleep stacked bars (changes), body battery (stays), stress (stays).
5. Section "Training": activities stacked bars (stays, full width), daily activity score bars in MET-min with the 600 target (changes). The three-colour daily-activity stack and the fitness-trends chart go.

**390.** Strip cells stack one per row (Recovery, Load, Fitness); the contributors list stays under the meter. Charts go single column, 220 px high; legend collapses to the active series only. Section order is unchanged (page summary → strip → load → recovery → training).

**Charts: stay** activities, recovery series, ACWR, body battery, stress. **Change** sleep (score line off, score moves to the chart header), daily activity (one MET-min series with the 600 target). **Go** the fitness-trends z-score chart entirely (replaced by the strip's sparklines, no tab), the three-colour activity stack.

## Copy

Numbers in braces are illustrative placeholders for the format, not measured values.

- Page summary: "Recovery {recovery}/100 · load {acwr} ACWR, {zone_relation} the 0.8 floor · RHR {rhr_delta} bpm and HRV {hrv_delta} ms over 14 d"

## Pass criteria

1. The first screen at 1440 shows a summary sentence containing three live numbers and no filler subtitle anywhere on the page.
2. Recovery shows "/100", the 40 and 70 ticks and a marker; each contributor has a bar and a denominator.
3. Training load shows the zone band with a marker; "{acwr}" is never the only thing on its card.
4. No card has a coloured left edge; no hue appears on a single-metric neutral chart.
5. Every chart header carries a value with unit, a comparison and the period.
6. Sleep chart has one unit (hours) and carries the score in its header; daily activity has one series in MET-min and a 600 target line; no fitness-trends chart exists on the page.
7. At 390 the strip stacks to one column and nothing truncates a value.
8. A null reading shows "—", never 0, and the line breaks at gaps.

## Wave 19 rounds

Five shot rounds (`w19-garmin-health-r1` … `r5`), each scored by a fresh `basalt-design-reviewer` except r4 (a verification shot after the switch to basalt-ui 2.18's `Meter`/`BarList`). The harness writes no `metrics.json` and no hover/focus crops, so the reviewer marks every measured Craft item unverified and judges by eye; that is a harness gap, not a page defect.

**Round 1** — Reviewer: every component fail (Craft unverified); top defects: the load cell and the summary said {acwr_a} while the ACWR chart header said {acwr_b} (the cell read today's partial row, the chart hides it before 22:00); the verdict word "Undertrained" on the meter; inconsistent periods; a dead gap in the load cell; German "Surfen"; header facts wrapping with a leading dot at 390. Keel read: the strip already matches the KPI row's anatomy (caption + qualifier on one mono line, hero with muted unit, micro-visual); the page still read as seven same-shaped charts after it. Changed: the cell and the summary now use the same visibility filter as the chart; the meter captions became ranges instead of a verdict word; acute/chronic became a two-row `BarList` filling the cell; unit on every fitness delta; per-week comparison on Activities; English type labels; no-wrap on each fact so a separator never sits alone.

**Round 2** — Reviewer: summary, recovery, activities and daily activity pass; strip, ACWR card, sleep, stress fail. Defects: VO2max has no sparkline and a bare delta; ACWR line and divergence strip on different x ticks; decimal comma on the ACWR axis against dot values; unlabelled stress bands and sleep band; strip bar fills touching label text. Keel read: strip is calm but monotone grey. Changed: both plots share one `xDomain` and explicit weekly `xTicks`; ACWR axis formatted with dots; VO2max sparkline spans the whole window and a zero delta reads "unchanged".

**Round 3** — Reviewer: all fail on the unverified Craft items; visible: zones on the meters indistinguishable, no direction glyph on deltas, dead band in the ACWR plot, header clauses wrapping to orphan lines, 29/28/30 d period mismatch. Keel read: the cells hold up next to the Keel row; the charts below still lack Keel's per-chart micro-visuals. Changed: ACWR y floor 1.8; rebased onto master and switched to `basalt-ui` `Meter`/`BarList` (round 4 verified the render).

**Round 5** — Reviewer: criteria 1–4 and 6 pass; 5 fails (units missing on body battery and stress; periods count measured days so they differ between charts); 7 partial (legends do not collapse at 390); 8 partial (no single-null state visible on the live data). Changed since r3: zone tints on both meters (status colour is earned there), direction glyphs on deltas, headroom on the load `BarList` so values clear the fill, the shaded-band and zone clauses dropped from the headers, MET-min unit on the daily-activity mean, "/100" and "points" units on stress and body battery, `≥` in the recovery legend. Keel read: the strip is at the bar; the chart grid is correct and honest but a step quieter than Keel's composite cards.

**Pass criteria at the end:** 1 holds. 2 holds with one gap: the 40/70 cut-offs are the zone edges and are named in the caption, but there is no window-mean mark (see basalt friction); the mean is in the cell qualifier. 3 holds (zoned meter, marker, `acute`/`chronic` rows). 4 holds. 5 holds for value, unit and comparison; the period is "N d" counted over measured days, so it differs by one or two between charts. 6 holds. 7 partial: the strip stacks and nothing truncates at 390; legends do not collapse to the active series. 8 holds for lines and bars (gaps, never 0) and for every formatter (`—`, unit-tested); no single-null reading exists in the live data to see it.

**Left open:** legend collapse at 390 (a basalt chart legend option, not a local workaround); the divergence strip shares x ticks with the ACWR line but still draws its own date axis; the 600 target line on daily activity carries no label (`refLines` has no label prop); sleep plots awake time as negative hours as the concept specifies.

### basalt friction

- `Meter`: a second mark on the track (the window mean on the recovery meter) — closest shipped prop is `target`, which already marks the zone boundary use; the mean went into the qualifier text.
- `Meter`: multiple `ticks` (40 and 70 as marks); zone edges stand in for them.
- `BarList`: a per-row `max` so each contributor is points of its own weight; contributors share one 0–40 scale and the denominator is in `secondary`, so a full 25-point contributor fills 62 % of the track.
- `ChartCard`: a `facts` slot (see `FactsLine`); `Bars`/`Lines`: a `refLines[].label` for the 600 target; chart legends: collapse to the active series at compact width.
