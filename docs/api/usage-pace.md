## Usage page data: credit pace, router, demand (v7)

The Claude API credit pools the inference gateway pays from (agent-workflows `docs/gateway.md`; accounts `iu` $200 Ultra, `me` $100, `me2` $100; the cycles differ and the credit expires with each). The gateway and usage-tracker write, Argo only reads: gateway rows are `usage.records` with `source='gateway'` (`billing='api_credit'` + `account` for a pool, `billing='iu'` + `api_key` for the IU passthrough), `lane_detail {caller, pool, workflow, step, purpose, fallback, costEstimated?, costEstimatedUsd?, routeFallback?, origin?}` (`USAGE_ROWS` projects it); `usage.credit_cycles` (`account, cycle_start, cycle_end, grant_usd, metered_usd, reconciled_usd, unspent_usd, closed_at`), `usage.gateway_picks` (`ts, caller, session, picked, reason, machine, request_id`) and `usage.platform_cost` (`account, day, model, api_key_id, cost_usd, refreshed_at`: each org's own daily spend at list price, lagging hours, **including spend made outside the gateway**). Each of the three tables may be missing or unreadable: every read is guarded and degrades to a decided empty shape, never a 404. The v6 `/usage/credits/{pools,picks,cache}` are gone; `pace` and `router` replace them (spec `docs/design/usage-tracking.md` v7, Gaps 1, 2 and the cause split).

- `GET /usage/pace` — the verdict band and the pool nodes, as **state** (no range). Pure arithmetic in `src/lib/usage-pace.ts` (tests `src/lib/usage-pace.test.ts`), reads in `src/routes/usage-pace.ts`. Per pool (`iu`, `me`, `me2` first; a pool without a running cycle is listed `enabled: false`): `spentUsd` = Σ over the cycle's UTC days of `max(metered_day, reported_day)` (the gateway reconciles the same way), `leftUsd`, `daysLeft`, `requiredUsdPerDay`, `evalReserveUsd` (grant × days left ÷ cycle days, the gateway's formula) and `evalAvailableUsd`, the burn-down `days`, `outsideUsd` (what the report holds on days the gateway carried nothing) and `previous` (the newest closed cycle). `rate` is the production rate R: credit-eligible Claude demand (a pool, or IU by fallback) with purpose ≠ `eval` over the last 7 × 24 h clipped to the first gateway row (`thin` under 24 h), E the same over evals. The **waterfall** drains the enabled pools in cycle-end order at R (`projection.{drawsFrom,dryAt,unspentAtEndUsd}`); `spill` is the stretch from the last pool running dry to the soonest renewal in Claude list dollars (never IU cost); `verdict` is `over` | `under` (a pool expires with more than max($5, 5 % of grant)) | `on-pace` | `no-pool`; `claudeOnIuUsd` is Claude work that reached IU since the cutover (picks `claude-code` / `no-pool` / `drained-retry` plus `lane_detail.routeFallback` rows); `personalKey` is the IU personal key's cap and `underCapOn`. A cycle with `status` `unknown-end` has no projection; `awaiting-reconcile` (ended, not closed) has no balance.
- `GET /usage/router?range` — `picks` (`gateway_picks` per UTC day × target × reason; the target is a pool id, `iu-api` or `refused`; a drained retry writes two picks), `iu` (gateway rows billed to the IU key per day × cause: `design`, `eval`, `claude-code`, `forced`, `credit-fallback`, `route-fallback`; its total equals the gateway's IU-key rows over the range and the Athena key never counts) and `cache` (hit rate per caller × target). `picksAvailable` false means `picks` is empty and the `iu` causes are unverified; `routeFallbackMeasured` false means `route-fallback` reads 0 because agw does not mark it yet (spec Gap 4).
- `GET /usage/demand?range&by=workflow|step|model|origin|caller|repo&workflow&purpose[]&payer[]&top` — gateway demand (plus agw's own media rows, never both for one call) per key: cost, fresh and cache-read tokens, requests, runs, `byPurpose` (`prod`, `eval`, `verify`, `other`) and `byPayer`; `totals` over every key. `by=origin` reads `lane_detail.origin` and is unset until agw forwards its caller (Gap 5).
- `GET /usage/split?by=lane` — the stored Claude Code lane (`interactive`, `wave_mother`, `lead`, `worker`, `fan_worker`, `subagent`, `hermes`, `iris`, `agw_workflow`, `other`) with its list value and fresh tokens per payer, i.e. per account. `GET /usage/iu-budget` gained `underCapOn` (the first day the rolling 30-day sum drops to the cap at the last 7 days' mean).

Dashboard: the **Quotas** tab (`view=flow`) reads `pace`, `quota`, `quota-latest`, `iu-budget` (both keys) for its cards and `router`, `demand`, `split?by=lane`, `timeseries` once its Routing and demand disclosure opens; **Explore** reads the kept analytics endpoints (`timeseries`, `split`, `rhythm`, `flow`).
